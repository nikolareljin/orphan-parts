# Optional establishing clips (Runway)

For context only: a few seconds of atmosphere at the start (S01, S02) when the owner's own
footage is not available. Never for anything the app does, never imitating its screens, never
showing a repair that worked. A clip from here carries the line *Illustrative clip, generated
with Runway* in the bottom right for its whole duration, and is listed in `assets.md`.

Before using any: check the plan's current terms for use in a public video and for a
watermark. Text-to-video only; no reference images of real products or people.

**R1, a kitchen routine (for S01, 4 to 6 s)**

> Early morning kitchen counter, soft window light, a plain unbranded drink mixer base and a
> tall glass, a small bag of ground coffee, steam from a kettle in the background, slow dolly
> in, shallow depth of field, natural colors, no people, no text, no logos, no screens.

**R2, a drawer of unused electronics (for S02, 4 to 6 s)**

> An open wooden drawer full of old laptops, tablets, coiled cables and chargers, top-down,
> slow pan left to right, warm indoor light, realistic, slightly dusty, no brand names, no
> text, no screens turned on, no people.

**R3, a living room corner (optional, before S11, 3 s)**

> A quiet living room corner with an empty low wooden coffee table, evening lamp light, slow
> push in, realistic, no people, no screens, no text, no logos.

Do not use a clip that shows a brand, a readable label, a hand doing a repair, an arcade
machine, a phone or computer screen, or anything that could be taken for the app.

# Muse Wonder

Not used in this version. Its ID is a setting (`MODEL_WONDER`), listed by `GET /api/version` and
checked by `./dev check-api`, and its published rates are in the client's price table, so a call
to it would be priced correctly; no feature makes one. The jobs it was considered for, planning
a comparison across several candidates and drafting a category profile from example photos, are
on the [roadmap](../project/roadmap.md). Muse Spark does every reasoning job today; see
[Muse Spark](muse-spark.md).

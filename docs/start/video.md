# Demo video

The demo video (2:52) shows the app as it was built during the hackathon: a worn frappé maker
agitator traced to its drink mixer, the maker's part found on the web and checked against the
part's rules, then the unused electronics in a drawer turned into builds, a price and a
Marketplace draft, and the Android app sending photos to the same inventory.

- Every answer on screen comes from the app on Meta's models (Muse Spark, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice
  Transcribe, Muse Image) or from their recordings, which the demo replays at no cost.
- The narrator is a synthetic voice. The voice note in the selling shot is synthetic too, read
  from the demo set's stated condition, and is transcribed by Muse Voice Transcribe.
- The photos are the owner's own, with serial numbers, codes and labels' private details
  blacked out.
- The video is made with the repository's own kit: `./dev video` (see `video/README.md`).

# Examples: what the models produced

Every picture on this page is the app showing an answer a Meta model gave for the demo set: the
owner's own photos of two drawers of idle parts, sent once to the models on the Model API and
recorded. `./dev demo` and `./dev docs-shots` replay those recordings, so the text and the
drawings are the models' own answers, replayed at no cost. The
[hosted demo](https://orphan-parts.pages.dev) shows the same content read-only to a visitor
without the access code; the pictures marked *visitor* are that view.

Which model wrote what: **Muse Spark** reads labels, writes the Decide cards, the proposals,
the price guidance, the Marketplace drafts, the search plans and the fit readings;
**SAM 3.1** finds the item, the label and the connector on a photo; **Muse Image** draws how a
build or a reuse could look; **Muse Voice Transcribe** turns a spoken note into text (shown in
[Voice notes and video capture](../guides/voice-and-video.md)). The rules, not a model, decide
what is accepted, what fits and what goes into a build pack.

## What should I do with it?

The **Decide** step: Muse Spark writes three cards for one item and the owner's rated skills
(keep and fix, reuse it with what else is owned, sell or give away), recommends one, and the
owner's choice is kept as the item's plan. A reuse card can be drawn by Muse Image, and an item
can be drawn as what it could become. Three items of the demo set, as a visitor reads them.

## Buyer flow: find and rank

A search plan by Muse Spark, written for a part the NAS still needs (a processor for the
ASRock board's AM4 socket): phrasings, each a Marketplace search link with the top price, why each one
finds listings the others miss, and the match pattern.

![Plan the search for the NAS processor: four Muse Spark phrasings as Marketplace links with Copy and why, the pattern and the words to skip](../assets/shots/examples/30-nas-cpu-plan.png)

The frappé maker's worn agitator needs an all-metal rod. **Search the web** (Muse Spark with
web search) found the maker's part, OEM 990010200, on several shops; every find is ranked with its price and link, by the rules, no model call.

![The listings brought back, ranked: the pages found on the web with their prices and links, each with Check fit](../assets/shots/examples/31-agitator-ranked.png)

**Check fit** on one: Muse Spark reads the page into values, the profile's rules decide
(*needs evidence*: no rod diameter on the page), and the questions to ask the seller.

![Check fit on the goodmans.net page: needs evidence, no hard rule could be decided, and the questions for the seller](../assets/shots/examples/32-agitator-fit.png)

The NAS is missing a power supply: its search, run on the web.

![The NAS power supply search: two ATX power supplies found on the web, ranked, each with its price and link](../assets/shots/examples/33-nas-psu-search.png)

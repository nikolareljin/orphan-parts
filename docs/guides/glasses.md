# Meta glasses

Not in this version. The hands-free path goes through the [Android app](android.md), which is
built: capture into the inventory and hold to talk. Glasses would reach the backend through
that app, for a capture by voice and a short answer read back; no part of that is wired in, and
the app does not use a wearables toolkit. It stays on the [roadmap](../project/roadmap.md); the
phone flow is complete on its own.

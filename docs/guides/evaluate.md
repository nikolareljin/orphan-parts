# Find and rank

Say what you need, in your own words or as a short profile: the job it has to do, the specs that
matter, the budget. Search returns candidates ranked against that need with a fit score. For any
candidate, paste the listing's text or upload its screenshot and get the listed specs beside the
known ones (with source and date), the gaps and conflicts, a deal check from the photos and the
asking price, a suitability verdict with reasons, the questions to ask the seller, and a comparison
with the items you saved.

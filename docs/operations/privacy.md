# Privacy

- Photos are stripped of EXIF and GPS metadata on ingest, before anything else happens.
- A photo sent for a reading is read in memory and dropped with the request; when you save the item, the web form keeps those photos with it under `DATA_DIR/items/`, and an item read from a capture keeps the capture's photos the same way; they go when you delete the item. The photo a measurement was made on is kept, with the measurement, on the item; a capture from the phone (photos, clip, note) is kept under `DATA_DIR/captures/` until you delete it from the Inbox.
- What leaves the machine: crops and prompts, the listing brief (the confirmed values and the details you typed: condition, faults, tests, accessories, pickup, price) the price brief (the same values and details, the currency and the place you give, which the Model API also turns into web searches) voice recordings, the listing text or screenshot you bring back for a search, and the build brief (your preferences and, through the tools the model calls, the items a build may use with their fields, condition, faults, tests, accessories and parts) and the uses brief (the item's name and type, its condition, faults, tests and accessories, the values you accepted, your interests, skills and budget, and the labels of the project templates it fills; never its location, its notes, a serial number, a photo or a search) go to the Meta Model API; the picture brief (the item's type, its accepted values without any that identify it or you, its condition and faults, and the use you typed) goes to Muse Image only when you click **Draw it**, never a serial number, a MAC address, an IMEI, the location, the notes or a photo; the build pack, the outcome and its share text are written by the rules on this machine and sent nowhere; the phone app sends its photos (re-encoded on the phone, pixels only), its clips and its recordings to your backend and nowhere else, and keeps the backend URL and the access code in its private storage; a capture stays under `DATA_DIR/captures/` until you delete it, the clip as the phone wrote it (its own metadata included), and reading a capture sends its photos and its note to the Meta Model API, never the clip; nothing goes anywhere else. A listing's link is kept as you gave it and never opened. With an access code set, the browser keeps one session cookie that holds no account and nothing about you; it ends after `DEMO_SESSION_HOURS` or when you leave. Codes issued with `./dev keys` are kept as hashes with a label; the log notes the label when a session opens, never the code. Without the code a visitor is the demo: the photos sent to the four stateless tries are answered from the recordings, never sent to the Model API, and dropped with the request.
- No accounts, no tracking, no third-party scripts in the web app.

!!! note
    Nothing is purged on a timer: `RETENTION_HOURS` has no job behind it yet. What is kept is
    listed above, and goes when you delete it.
